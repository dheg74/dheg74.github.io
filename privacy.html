AP Agent Privacy Policy

AP Agent is used only by DMSC Cakes on its own billing mailbox. It is not offered to the public.

The app reads emails and attachments in that mailbox to identify vendor invoices, saves invoice files to company Dropbox storage, and applies labels in the mailbox. It does not send email or permanently delete messages.

Email content is processed by the Anthropic API to classify invoices. No data is sold or shared with anyone else.
